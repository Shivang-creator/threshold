## Deviation — flagged, not silent: the ground shader's `fall` term is clamped to 2.5
DESIGN §6.6's formula, implemented verbatim, blows out badly at the actual camera/lamp positions
from §6.2/§6.4. KEY_POS (y=0.62) sits close enough to the visible drawable disc that the lamp's
1/d² `fall` term reaches ~6.5 directly under the lamp and ~5.5 at the camera's specular
mirror-reflection point (world ≈ (0.86, 0, 1.45)) — both inside the visible area. The "broad warm
pool" ambient term alone (`fall * 0.06`) crosses Bloom's 0.28 `luminanceThreshold` anywhere within
about a 1-unit radius of the lamp's ground projection, and `mipmapBlur` bloom reads that as a
frame-dominating orange blob, not the subtle "near-black wet stone" DESIGN describes.

I verified this was genuinely the ground shader (not a grain bug) by forcing `gl_FragColor =
vec4(uStone900, 1.0)` and confirming the blob disappeared entirely, then hand-derived the hotspot
as `KEY_POS` mirrored through the flat ground along the camera ray. This is an interaction between
three independently-specified DESIGN sections (camera, light rig, ground formula), not a
transcription error on my part.

**Fix applied:** a single shared cap, `min(fall, 2.5)`, used for both the specular and ambient
terms — 2.5 sits just above the value `fall` naturally takes at the kolam's own centre (~2.36), so
most of the visible disc is close to unchanged; only the zone directly under the lamp is pulled
back into range. **This is a deviation from DESIGN's literal formula, not a personal restyle** —
full numeric derivation is in `ground.frag.js`'s comment at the `fall` line.

**Still not fully resolved — say so plainly:** even after the 2.5 clamp, `composition-after.png`
still shows a visibly warm glow near the lamp, more prominent than DESIGN's "near-black wet stone"
framing suggests. It's a large, real improvement over the flat unlit "before" (lighting, sheen,
vignette shape are all now genuinely present) but it is not yet the subtle look §3.3 describes.
**needs-decision for pc-designer:** either accept 2.5 as shipped, tune it further (I'd try 1.8–2.0
next), or retune `KEY_INT`/the `0.06` ambient scale in DESIGN itself so the formula doesn't need a
render-side cap at all. I did not chase this further per the Crew Chief's stop instruction.

## The backtick bug — note for everyone writing GLSL-in-template-literal files
`grain.frag.js`/`grain.vert.js`/`ground.frag.js`/`ground.vert.js` are all plain JS template literals
holding GLSL source. **A literal backtick anywhere inside that string — including inside a `//` GLSL
comment quoting a variable name — terminates the JS string early** and breaks the build with a
confusing "Expected a semicolon" error pointing at the wrong line. I hit this once (comment read
`` the value `fall` naturally takes ``) and broke the dev server; the Crew Chief fixed it
(single-quoted the variable name instead) before I could. **Never use backticks inside a comment
that lives inside a template-literal shader string** — use single quotes or plain text instead.
